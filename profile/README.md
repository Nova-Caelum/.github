## Nova Caelum

**Individual Empowerment Systems**

*More room for what only you can do.*

We build systems that give people time back, bring new capabilities within reach, and support them as their lives change. Whether you are starting your first build or coordinating a growing team of agents, these tools help you turn ideas into working systems, understand the decisions along the way, and keep the work tied to your goals.

Everything here began in our own working system. We use these tools to plan, build, review, and check our work, then refine them so you can bring them into yours.

### Find your starting point

| Product | What it helps you do |
| --- | --- |
| [**Technical Cofounder**](https://github.com/Nova-Caelum/technical-cofounder) | Give your idea an AI senior dev team. Work with a technical cofounder to shape the plan, an engineer to build it, a DevOps lead to review the work and support the system, and a forward-deployed engineer to help you get started, learn, and extend your team. For solo builders and the build-curious. Hyperspace Engine installs with the team. |
| [**Hyperspace Engine**](https://github.com/Nova-Caelum/hyperspace-engine) | Turn a goal into a plan and follow it through to use. A shared task graph keeps the work visible, while deterministic checks and intelligent judgment help assess the evidence at each stage. Review the results and step in where your judgment matters. |
| [**Caelos**](https://github.com/Nova-Caelum/Caelos) | Think, build, and carry work forward with your agents in one place. Follow the plan and coordinate your team without living in a terminal. |
| [**no-mistakes**](https://github.com/Nova-Caelum/no-mistakes) | Give your agents three skills for checking assumptions, working through complex problems, and verifying results before claiming completion. Installs as a plugin. |
| [**fresh-eyes**](https://github.com/Nova-Caelum/fresh-eyes) | Get a separate perspective on the work. A reviewer starts with a clean context, reads your review packet, and returns a verdict grounded in the evidence. |
| [**devops-lead**](https://github.com/Nova-Caelum/devops-lead) | Review pull requests through GitHub Actions, with automated review and merge handling and escalation to a person when needed. |

### How we build

- **Your goals guide the work.** The tools help you make decisions, understand tradeoffs, and build capabilities of your own.
- **Evidence before completion.** Checks and review support completion claims. Keep the evidence with the work and unresolved questions visible.
- **Used here, shared with you.** These tools come from our working systems. We keep improving installation, guidance, and compatibility so they fit more people's needs.

### What's next

Technical Cofounder's roadmap includes Codex and Hermes compatibility alongside Claude Code, simpler setup for its optional research tools, capabilities from Caelos as they mature, and more tools for engineering and verification. These are directions, not dates; each repository carries its current requirements and roadmap.
